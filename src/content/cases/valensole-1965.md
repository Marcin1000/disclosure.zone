## The French procedure again

As at Cussac and Trans-en-Provence, the value of Valensole comes from **the gendarmerie arriving the
same day and writing a protocol.** France converted UAP reporting into an ordinary policing task, and
that single administrative decision produced case files no other country of the era can match.

## What the physical component actually is

Ground marks were documented immediately. The claim that the lavender in that patch grew abnormally
for years afterwards is weaker: it was observed and reported, but **no controlled agronomic study was
run at the time**, with sampling and comparison plots.

That gap is the difference between Valensole and Trans-en-Provence sixteen years later. By 1981 GEPAN
existed, and when a comparable event occurred the samples were taken properly. Valensole is what the
same case looks like without an agency behind it.
