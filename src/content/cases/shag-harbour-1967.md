## What the documents actually establish

**They establish:** the report, multiple witness sightings, the launch of a rescue operation, the
absence of any missing aircraft, and the formal closure of the case without identification.

**They do not establish:** anything from the later narrative about a recovery, an underwater
military operation, or the presence of submarines.

That distinction matters particularly at Shag Harbour, because the case is often presented as "a
Canadian Roswell with government evidence". Government evidence exists — but it concerns **an
investigation**, not a recovery of anything.
