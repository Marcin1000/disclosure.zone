## The best proof that the eye is wrong about speed

GOFAST looks exactly like an object flying centimetres above the water at breathtaking speed. It is
not.

The data needed to work it out is **on the footage itself**: the viewing angle and the range to
target are displayed on the HUD. Once applied, the object turns out to be high above the surface
rather than near it, and its actual airspeed is on the order of the wind.

The sense of velocity comes from **the motion of the observing aircraft.** This is parallax — the
same effect by which roadside trees "fly past" while distant mountains stand still.

## Why the case stays in the database with an anomaly score of 1/5

Because it is one of three videos whose authenticity the Pentagon confirmed — and it is still
presented as evidence of non-terrestrial performance. Separating those two things — **an authentic
recording** and **an extraordinary object** — is this database's principal job.
