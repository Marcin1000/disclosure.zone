## The physical-trace case Americans should point to instead of Roswell

Delphos has what Roswell does not: **material that was sampled, analysed and retained.**

The ring's soil repelled water so strongly that it could be floated. The effect persisted for years
and was documented by more than one laboratory. Photographs were taken at the time.

## The fungal explanation, taken seriously

Certain soil fungi form rings and produce hydrophobic mycelium. This is a real phenomenon, it is
common, and it is the leading explanation.

Its difficulties are specific: the reported sharpness of the ring's boundary, the depth to which the
hydrophobicity extended, and the reported luminosity at the time of the event. None of these is
decisive against the fungal reading, and honest treatment has to say so.

## Why it sits where it does

Single young witness, no radar, no corroborating observer at the moment of the event. But
**the trace outlived the witness's account by years and could be measured by strangers** — which is
exactly the property that makes Trans-en-Provence valuable, and exactly what abduction cases can
never offer.
