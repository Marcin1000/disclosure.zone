## Significance: the state sent soldiers to watch

Operação Prato is one of very few cases worldwide in which a government **deployed a military unit
into the field with orders to systematically document UAP.** Only the American Project Twinkle and
the Norwegian Hessdalen expedition are comparable.

A large volume of material was gathered. Its instrumental quality, however, is low: the photographs
are poor, there is no radar, there are no range measurements. What we received is an extensive,
well-documented body of **accounts** — not a body of measurements.

## The medical component

Reports of injuries to residents are the most heavily exploited element of this case and the least
documented. No publicly available medical record permits the described symptoms to be attributed to
any specific cause.
