## The best-attested testimonial case in the corpus

Boianai has something no other testimony-only case has: **the account was written down immediately
and countersigned by all thirty-eight witnesses.** That eliminates, at a stroke, the drift and
narrative accretion that ruins Roswell, Rendlesham and Westall.

The witness was a mission priest with no incentive toward the sensational, and the co-signers
included local staff and villagers with no shared cultural template for flying saucers in 1959.

## Why it still scores as testimony

There is no instrument. No photograph, no radar, no trace. The entire case rests on what
thirty-eight people said they saw — recorded impeccably, but still only that.

## The Venus objection, stated fairly

The sceptical explanation is Venus, which was bright and correctly placed. It accounts for a
stationary luminous object over hours. It accounts far less well for the reported apparent structure
and for the figures. Neither side can close the argument, because the one thing that would — a
photograph, a bearing measurement — was never taken.

Boianai is the ceiling of what human testimony alone can achieve. That ceiling is lower than people
expect, and knowing exactly where it sits is useful.
