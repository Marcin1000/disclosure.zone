## Why the documentation matters more than the sighting

JAL 1628 is in the first class not because the sighting is extraordinary — stronger ones exist. It is
here because **the FAA treated the event as an aviation incident and ran a full investigation**:
interviews with the entire crew, transcripts, radar printouts, controller position logs.

That makes it one of the few older cases that can be partially reconstructed today from documents
rather than from participants' memories.

## Where the case weakens

The FAA analysis did not conclusively establish that the radar returns came from a separate object.
A split return from the 747 itself was among the possibilities considered. Meanwhile an unusually
bright Jupiter–Mars pairing sat low on the horizon that night, in precisely the direction of the
first sightings.

## The element that holds up

Captain Terauchi described a very large structure seen against the lights of the city of Fairbanks —
an observation with a spatial reference, not a point on a black sky. That single fragment of the
account is far harder to explain with a planet than anything else in the case.
