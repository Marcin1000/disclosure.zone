## Why a state endorsed a photograph

Trindade is unusual in one specific respect: the **head of state personally backed the release** of
UFO photographs taken from a naval vessel. Nothing comparable happened in the United States.

That is a fact about institutional behaviour, not about the object. It tells us the Brazilian Navy
found no reason to call the images fraudulent after examining the negatives — a meaningfully
stronger statement than “we cannot identify it”.

## Why the score stays moderate

The photographer had prior experience in trick photography, which is a legitimate and serious
objection. The negatives' subsequent history is contested. And the fundamental limit of any
photograph applies here as it does at McMinnville: **without a known range, the image is consistent
with a small nearby object and a large distant one.**

The case is strong on institutional handling and weak on physical determinability. That combination
is common in this corpus and worth naming clearly.
