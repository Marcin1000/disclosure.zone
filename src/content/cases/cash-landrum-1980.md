## The strongest and weakest element are the same thing

**Strongest:** this case went through a federal court. The witnesses submitted to examination, bore
the costs, and produced medical documentation. This is not an internet anecdote.

**Weakest:** the medical record proves the witnesses were ill. It does not prove the object made
them ill. Causation remains unestablished, and the court dismissed the case on exactly that basis.

The motif of a dozen CH-47 escorts is the most intriguing element: were it real, it would imply the
commitment of substantial domestic forces — and therefore flight documentation that was never found.
