## The most important recent case, and not for the reason people think

Nothing anomalous happened here. That is exactly the point.

The first object was recovered, examined and identified. The other three were engaged with missiles,
never recovered, and never identified — and the reason they were seen at all is documented: **NORAD
deliberately adjusted its radar filters** to stop screening out slow, small, high-altitude returns.

## What that single fact does to the whole field

For decades, air defence radar has been tuned to discard exactly the signature that most UAP reports
describe: slow, small, high, non-manoeuvring. When the filters came off, the sky immediately filled
with objects nobody had been seeing.

This is the strongest available evidence for a deflationary reading of the modern UAP surge — and,
simultaneously, for the claim that the sky was never being properly monitored. **Both readings are
supported by the same fact**, and anyone using this event should say which one they mean.

## Where the category boundary broke

Three objects were destroyed by advanced fighters over North America and never identified. Under any
consistent definition they are unidentified anomalous phenomena. They are also, almost certainly,
balloons.

That is where "UAP" stopped being a question about visitors and became a question about airspace
awareness — which is the frame AARO now works in, and the frame the Virginia coast case of 2024
belongs to.
