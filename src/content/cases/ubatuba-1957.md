## Why this is a model case — negatively

Ubatuba shows that **laboratory analysis of material of unknown origin has no evidential value**,
however precise the analysis itself.

We can establish with great precision that a sample is magnesium of purity X. We cannot establish
in any way that the sample came from an object that exploded above a beach — because there is no
documented chain of custody from the site to the laboratory.

The same problem recurs exactly sixty years later with the celebrated magnesium-bismuth sample
examined by AARO. The conclusion is identical, and it is one of the most important in the field:
**without a chain of custody there is no material evidence.**
